country: KSA, UAE, Egypt, Jordan
lastUpdated: 2026-08-01

## Purpose
Issue an official salary certificate.

## Steps
1. Verify employee identity in BambooHR.
2. Confirm basic salary and allowances from payroll, not from chat.
3. HR prepares the certificate on letterhead in Arabic and English if requested.
4. Standard turnaround is two working days.
5. Bank letters use the IBAN on file. Missing IBAN blocks issuance.
