## Purpose
How coordinators answer Nitaqat and Saudization questions.

## Steps
1. Identify the employee's job band and current Nitaqat colour for the entity.
2. New KSA hires in restricted occupations need Saudization review before offer.
3. Iqama issuance for non-Saudis follows Ministry of Human Resources quotas.
4. Do not promise a visa or transfer until HR Compliance signs off.
5. Log gaps if the employee asks for a quota number the agent cannot see.
