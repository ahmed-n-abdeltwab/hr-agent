## Purpose
Coordinator briefing for Saudi labour-law questions raised in the pilot.

## Steps
1. Confirm the employee entity is Gulf Industries KSA.
2. Annual leave is 21 days under 5 years of service and 30 days from year 5.
3. Sick leave follows 30 calendar days at 100% pay then 60 days at 75%.
4. Probation is up to 90 days unless the contract says otherwise.
5. Escalate wage claims and dismissals to HR Legal. The agent must not guess.
