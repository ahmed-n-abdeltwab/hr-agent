## Purpose
Country-specific probation for the conglomerate.

## Steps
1. KSA: up to 90 days, extendable once in writing.
2. UAE: typically 6 months unless the contract is shorter.
3. Egypt: up to 3 months.
4. Jordan: up to 3 months.
5. Confirm hire date in BambooHR before telling an employee they are still on probation.
