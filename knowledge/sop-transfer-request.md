title: Transfer request SOP
country: KSA, UAE, Egypt, Jordan
lastUpdated: 2026-08-01

## Purpose
Move an employee between entities or locations.

## Steps
1. Collect current entity, target entity, and proposed date.
2. Manager of both sides must approve in writing.
3. HR updates BambooHR location, entity, and supervisor.
4. For KSA to KSA plant moves, refresh safety orientation.
5. Immigration follow-up is required for cross-border transfers.
