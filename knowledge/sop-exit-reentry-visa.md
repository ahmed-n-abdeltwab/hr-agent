title: Exit and re-entry visa process
country: KSA
lastUpdated: 2026-08-01

## Purpose
Single or multiple exit/re-entry visas for KSA residents.

## Steps
1. Confirm Iqama is valid for at least 30 days after planned return.
2. Manager approves leave dates first.
3. Coordinator submits Muqeem / Absher request with passport copy.
4. Fees are billed to the employee unless the travel is company-paid.
5. Cancel unused visas after return so Nitaqat records stay clean.
