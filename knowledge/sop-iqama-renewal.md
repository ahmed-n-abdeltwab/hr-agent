country: KSA

## Purpose
Renew resident identity (Iqama) before expiry.

## Steps
1. Run the daily Iqama job and treat 30 days or fewer as urgent.
2. Collect a fresh passport scan and medical insurance card.
3. Confirm no unpaid traffic or labour violations.
4. Submit renewal through Muqeem and update BambooHR customIqamaExpiry.
5. Alert the employee and HR in Arabic and English.
