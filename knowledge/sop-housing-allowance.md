## Purpose
Who receives housing allowance and how it is paid.

## Steps
1. Grade E and above receive a monthly housing allowance unless company housing is provided.
2. Plant roles in Jeddah may receive camp housing instead of cash.
3. UAE packages are inclusive; do not double-pay housing.
4. Changes to allowance are compensation changes and must go to HR.
5. Keep the policy text; do not invent new percentages in chat.
